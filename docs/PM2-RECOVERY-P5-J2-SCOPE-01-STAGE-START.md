## §9. Invalidation triggers

### 9.1 Existing E-J5 regime (preserved)

Per P5-JOURNAL-01 §3.2 E-J5, an accepted recovery-material absence attestation is invalidated by:

- Applicable material discovered for (H, A) within **or outside** the approved scope
- New activity on (H, A) that could produce applicable material
- Scope found to omit a relevant location (incomplete-coverage element)
- Keith revocation

J-2 criterion 6 independently records new activity after search and before attestation acceptance; such activity invalidates the attestation if it could have produced applicable material.

### 9.2 U-2-specific additional trigger (AMENDMENT-01 B.7)

U-2 acceptances carry one additional invalidation trigger: discovery of historical evidence (retained records, testimony, audit logs, recovered access records) that **materially changes** the basis for the U-2(e) acceptance. "Materially changes" means: evidence that identifies previously unknown operator accounts that had access to H, or evidence of previously unknown operator activity on H involving PM2 or 01C6A-class tooling, or evidence that the searched scope was inadequate due to previously unknown deployment patterns.

### 9.3 Revalidation after invalidation

A new attestation requires a new current-host scope approval **before any new search**, a new search under that approval, and a new U-2(e) acceptance if historical unknowns remain. The previous acceptance cannot be reinstated without a new process. Search is never performed before the new scope approval.

### 9.4 Triggers that already block before U-2(e)

- Any remaining U-1 unknown after the approved search
- E-J6 (inaccessible, corrupted, or incomplete current-path evidence)
- H1 or UNCLASSIFIED material within approved scope (criteria 4)
- Pending operations (U1-pending)
- This proposal being treated as approval or as U-2(e)
